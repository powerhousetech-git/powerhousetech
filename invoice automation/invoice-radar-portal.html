Connections
How invoices come in and how reminders go out.

        

          

            
G
Gmail
Connected · read-only

            
Auto-scans the inbox for incoming invoices & receipts. Read-only scope — Radar never sends or deletes from here. Prefer zero access? Switch to a forwarding address instead.

          

          

            
W
WhatsApp Business
Primary channel

            
Reminders go out over WhatsApp first — it gets read in minutes. Email & SMS are automatic fallbacks. (SMS requires DLT registration in India.)

          

          

            
Z
Zoho Books / Tally
Off — tap to sync

            
Already on Zoho or Tally? Turn this on and Radar rides on top — pulling invoices in and pushing payment status back, so you don't replace your books, you supercharge them.

          

          

            
▤
Master Google Sheet
Source of truth · synced
Open sheet ↗

            
Every invoice lives in one sheet you own. This portal is a live window onto it. At 2,000 invoices Radar auto-rolls over to a fresh sheet and archives the old one (higher tier) so it never slows down.
