What we actually delivered.

Card Capture

Snap a photo of a business card. Name, title, company, email, phone, and website are read, cleaned, checked for duplicates, and written into your central database — no manual typing.

Follow-Up Engine

When a lead lands — from a scanned card or your lead-scraping workflows — a personalized sequence starts from your own mailbox: an industry-aware intro, then timed nudges.

No duplicate mess

New cards are matched against existing contacts before anything is saved, so the same person doesn’t appear twice in the system.

No double-messaging

The follow-up engine tracks who has already been contacted. Nobody gets the same intro twice.

Built for exhibition floors

Designed for exporters and manufacturers who live on trade shows — electronics, auto components, textiles, pharma, handicrafts — where cards pile up faster than teams can type.

End-to-end handoff

Card Capture fills the database. The Follow-Up Engine works those leads. Together they close the gap between the booth handshake and a real conversation.
