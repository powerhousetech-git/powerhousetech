About

A small studio for teams done losing hours to busywork.

Powerhouse started as an automation practice serving Indian chartered accountants. The same patterns — spreadsheets, inboxes, manual hand-offs — were breaking backs in operations, sales, and finance teams everywhere.

Today we work with a small number of international clients at a time. We stay small on purpose: the person in discovery is the person shipping the code.

What we believe

Principles, not policies.

  • Outcomes over deliverables. We bill on scope, but measure on hours saved and errors avoided.
  • Small teams, senior people. No offshore hand-offs. No juniors thrown at your project.
  • Own the boring parts. Monitoring, docs, runbooks. Automation isn't done when the demo works.
  • Your stack, your data. We build in tools you trust. No lock-in, full ownership when we hand over.
