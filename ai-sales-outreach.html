How it works

A seven-stage pipeline.

From intake to inbox — each stage hands off to the next so nothing stalls.

  1. 01Lead intakeValidates prospects; rejects blanks, role addresses, bad formats.
  2. 02AI personalizationWrites a tailored opener, angle, and P.S. per prospect.
  3. 03SendSends from your address under the daily cap — only when sending is on.
  4. 04Follow-upAuto-sends after a set no-reply window.
  5. 05Reply scanCaptures responses against the right prospect.
  6. 06Reply triageClassifies each reply and drafts a suggested response.
  7. 07Deliverability guardianSuppresses bounced or complained addresses.
